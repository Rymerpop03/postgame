# Postgame — how to report a security problem.
#
# Format: RFC 9116. Served from https://postgame.app/.well-known/security.txt
#
# `Expires` is mandatory and must be in the future — a security.txt whose date has
# passed is not a valid one, and a stale contact address is worse than none, because
# somebody with a genuine finding will believe they have reported it. The deploy
# readiness check (api/tools/check_deploy_ready.py) refuses a deployment once this
# date is inside 30 days, so renewing it is a step you are told about rather than one
# you remember.

Contact: mailto:security@postgame.app
Expires: 2027-08-01T00:00:00.000Z
Preferred-Languages: en
Canonical: https://postgame.app/.well-known/security.txt

# What we would like

# Please give us a reasonable window to fix something before publishing it. We will
# not pursue anyone who reports in good faith, stays within their own account, and
# does not degrade the service for other people.

# In scope: postgame.app and img.postgame.app.
# Out of scope: denial of service, social engineering of anyone, physical attacks,
# spam, and findings that consist only of a scanner's output.
